Research

Research areas

Wireless and NextG security
Security analysis and defenses for Wi-Fi, connection-establishment protocols, wireless communications, connected devices, and next-generation networks.
Moving-target defense
Dynamic mechanisms that change models, configurations, and exposed attack surfaces to make reconnaissance and exploitation less reliable.
Adversarial machine learning
Attacks against learning-enabled wireless systems and robust learning methods for modulation classification, signal analysis, and cyber defense.
Formal and systematic analysis
Structured analysis of protocol states, pre-authentication behavior, and attacker capabilities to uncover vulnerabilities before deployment.
Experimental evaluation
Hands-on validation using software-defined radios, wireless testbeds, open datasets, and reproducible implementations.
