Research Projects

Cyber Deception and Adaptive Defense in Wireless Systems

This project investigates proactive security mechanisms that continuously adapt wireless-system behavior to create uncertainty for adversaries. The research develops and evaluates moving-target defenses, modulation obfuscation techniques, and adaptive strategies for countering modulation-classification and adversarial machine-learning attacks.

Selected publications

  • 2024

  • 2023

  • 2021

Trustworthy RF Intelligence

This project develops reliable artificial-intelligence methods for understanding radio-frequency signals in dynamic and previously unseen environments. The research explores language-guided and zero-shot modulation recognition to enable next-generation IoT receivers to identify new signal types without requiring labeled training examples for every modulation.

Selected publications

  • 2026

    I. C. Llorente and N. Hoque, “Language-Based Zero-Shot Modulation Recognition for Next-Generation IoT Receivers,” in Proceedings of the IEEE International Conference on Artificial Intelligence, Blockchain, and Internet of Things (AIBThings), Mount Pleasant, MI, Sep. 2026.

Wireless Protocol Security

This project systematically analyzes wireless protocols to identify security weaknesses that arise before authentication and during connection establishment. The research studies relay, spoofing, denial-of-service, and man-in-the-middle attacks in Wi-Fi systems and develops practical defenses that remain effective during concurrent connection attempts.

Selected publications

  • 2025

    N. Hoque and H. Rahbari, “Locking Down Relay and Spoofing Attacks during Concurrent Connection Establishments in 802.11ax,” IEEE Transactions on Dependable and Secure Computing, vol. 22, no. 6, pp. 7287–7301, Dec. 2025.

  • 2023

    N. Hoque and H. Rahbari, “Countering Relay and Spoofing Attacks in the Connection Establishment Phase of Wi-Fi Systems,” in Proceedings of the ACM Conference on Security and Privacy in Wireless and Mobile Networks (WiSec), pp. 275–285, Guildford, United Kingdom, May–June 2023.

  • 2022

    N. Hoque, H. Rahbari, and C. Rezendes, “Systematically Analyzing Vulnerabilities in the Connection Establishment Phase of Wi-Fi Systems,” in Proceedings of the IEEE Conference on Communications and Network Security (CNS), pp. 64–72, Austin, TX, Oct. 2022.

Datasets and Testbeds

Wi-Fi Pre-Authentication Attack and Defense Testbed

An experimental testbed for implementing, analyzing, and evaluating relay and spoofing attacks during the connection-establishment phase of Wi-Fi systems. The testbed supports reproducible experimentation with pre-authentication attacks and their corresponding defenses.

View the Wi-Fi pre-authentication testbed on GitHub

Obfuscated Wireless-Signal Dataset and Evaluation Testbed

A dataset of obfuscated wireless signals and an experimental testbed for evaluating modulation classification attacks, modulation-obfuscation methods, and countermeasures. The resource supports research on adversarial robustness and adaptive defenses for AI-enabled wireless systems.

View the wireless-signal dataset and testbed on GitHub
