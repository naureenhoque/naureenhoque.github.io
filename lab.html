Intelligent Cyber-Physical Systems Security (iCPSS) Lab

About Us

Welcome to the Intelligent Cyber-Physical Systems Security (iCPSS) Lab in the College of Computing at Grand Valley State University. Our research focuses on the security, privacy, resilience, and trustworthiness of intelligent cyber-physical systems. The iCPSS Lab is led by Dr. Naureen Hoque. For collaboration opportunities, please feel free to get in touch. Learn more about our research projects and publications.
