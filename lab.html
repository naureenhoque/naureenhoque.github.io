Intelligent Cyber-Physical Systems Security (iCPSS) Lab

About Us

Welcome to the Intelligent Cyber-Physical Systems Security (iCPSS) Lab in the College of Computing at Grand Valley State University.

Our research focuses on the security, privacy, resilience, and trustworthiness of wireless, intelligent, and cyber-physical systems.

The director of the iCPSS Lab is Dr. Naureen Hoque. For collaboration opportunities, please feel free to get in touch.
