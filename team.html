Research Team

We study how wireless, intelligent, and connected systems can remain secure, resilient, and trustworthy as attackers and technologies evolve.

Mission

Adaptive security for changing systems.

The lab connects rigorous security analysis with experimental validation and implementation. We are especially interested in defenses that change the attack surface, responsible use of artificial intelligence for cyber defense, and practical security for next-generation communications.

Core areas

Wireless and NextG security

Wi-Fi, wireless protocols, connected devices, and next-generation communication systems.

AI-powered cyber defense

Threat detection, moving-target defense, and cyber deception.

Adversarial machine learning

Robust models and adaptive defenses for learning-enabled systems.

Reproducible experimentation

Open datasets, software-defined radios, testbeds, and hands-on evaluation.

Faculty lead

Naureen Hoque, Ph.D.
