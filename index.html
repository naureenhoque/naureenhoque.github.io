Naureen Hoque, Ph.D.

I am an Assistant Professor in the College of Computing at Grand Valley State University. I earned my Ph.D. in Computing and Information Sciences from Rochester Institute of Technology and my M.S. in Computer Science, with distinction, from the University of Nebraska–Lincoln. My research interests include wireless and next-generation network security, adaptive and deception-based cyber defense, adversarial machine learning, and experimental security using real systems, open-source testbeds, and software-defined radios.
