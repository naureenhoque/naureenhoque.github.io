Naureen Hoque, Ph.D.

Cybersecurity · Wireless Systems · Artificial Intelligence

I develop adaptive defenses for wireless and intelligent systems, combining systematic security analysis with machine learning and hands-on experimentation.

My research examines exposed attack surfaces in Wi-Fi and next-generation wireless systems, moving-target and deception-based defenses, adversarial machine learning, and practical security evaluation using real systems and software-defined radios.

I joined the College of Computing at Grand Valley State University in 2025 after completing my Ph.D. in Computing and Information Sciences at Rochester Institute of Technology.

Research interests

Security for wireless, connected, and learning-enabled systems, with an emphasis on defenses that adapt as quickly as the threat landscape.

Wireless and NextG security

Systematic analysis and defense of Wi-Fi connection establishment, wireless protocols, connected vehicles, and emerging communication systems.

Adaptive cyber defense

Moving-target defense and cyber deception techniques that continually change the defender's exposed attack surface.

Adversarial machine learning

Understanding attacks on learning-enabled wireless systems and developing robust models for security-sensitive environments.

Experimental security

Reproducible datasets, open-source testbeds, software-defined radios, and practical evaluation of security mechanisms.
